# Provenance

Creator Workflow Kit adapts Ivan's shared Stop Slop entrypoint, derived from `hardikpandya/stop-slop` at commit `8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9` under its MIT license. The entrypoint treats patterns as editing guidance and preserves factual meaning rather than imposing grammar bans or self-ratings. The three supporting reference files and original `LICENSE` retain the upstream material and copyright notice. Repository instructions and explicit user requirements take precedence.
